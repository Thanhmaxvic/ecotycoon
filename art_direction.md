# Eco-Tycoon Art Direction

- **Style**: Casual, clean, 2.5D isometric cartoon/vector style. Similar to SimCity or FarmVille but with an environmental focus.
- **Palette**: 
  - **Polluted**: Desaturated greys, browns, murky dark teals for water.
  - **Clean**: Vibrant emerald greens, bright sky blues, pops of color from flowers.
- **Key Assets**:
  - `tile_polluted`: Greyish-brown isometric dirt tile.
  - `tile_clean`: Lush green isometric grass tile with small flowers.
  - `water_polluted`: Dark, oily isometric water tile.
  - `water_clean`: Sparkling clear isometric water tile.
  - `central_filter`: Large isometric machine, clean white/metal with glowing blue glass tubes.
  - `solar_panel`: Blue/black isometric panels on a stand.
  - `wind_turbine`: Tall white isometric turbine with blades.
  - `greenhouse`: Glass-domed isometric building with green plants visible inside.
  - `conveyor_belt`: Dark grey industrial conveyor belt section (isometric).
- **UI**: Clean, modern rounded corners, translucent panels, bright primary buttons.
