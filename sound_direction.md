# Eco-Tycoon Sound Direction

- **Music**: 
  - **Main Theme**: Lo-fi, upbeat, acoustic guitar or marimba-driven track. Chill and constructive mood. (15s loop)
- **SFX**:
  - `build_click`: A satisfying wooden or metallic "thud" or "click" when placing buildings.
  - `clean_progress`: A sparkly, magical wind chime sound when a new area is cleaned.
  - `ui_hover`: Subtle soft pop for menu interactions.
- **Playback**: First-gesture unlock, looping background music with volume toggle in UI.
